About

Studio Philosophy

jorm.objects exists at the intersection of craft and concept. Every piece begins with a material — its weight, its grain, its willingness to be shaped — and a question about how objects occupy space.

We believe in structural honesty: nothing decorative that doesn't serve, nothing hidden that should be seen. Our work embraces tension, asymmetry, and the beauty of visible construction.

Process

Each project begins with material studies and hand sketches before moving to prototyping. We work directly with fabricators and artisans, maintaining hands-on involvement from concept through final production.

Limited editions and one-offs are produced in our workshop. Production pieces are manufactured in partnership with small-batch facilities across Northern Europe.
